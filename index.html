    Built to be opened every day

    Nexus runs in your browser, installs like an app and remembers how you arranged everything.

    • See what's running

      Every app gets a status light, checked when you open Nexus. The header counts how many are up.

    • Pin, group and reorder

      Pin favorites to a quick-launch strip and press 1 to 9 to open them. Sort apps into collapsible groups and drag to rearrange.

    • Four ways to look

      Switch between cards, compact cards, icons or a plain list, and resize the grid to fit your screen.

    • Installs like an app

      Add it to your home screen on iPhone or Android, or install it on Mac and Windows. It opens full screen and loads fast.

    • Your data stays with you

      Everything is stored in your browser. Network calls happen only when you run an AI feature with your own Groq key or scan apps for fresh details.

    • Back up in one file

      Export your apps, icons, notes and per-app data as a single backup, then restore it on any device. Build a portfolio poster to share.
